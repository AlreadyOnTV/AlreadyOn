A few things to know.

Does AlreadyOn include movies or TV shows?

No. You provide your own media library. AlreadyOn organizes playback into scheduled channels; it does not supply entertainment content or subscriptions.

Can I use it without an Android device?

Yes. The WebUI provides a browser guide and playback. Android phone/tablet and Google TV apps are additional ways to watch. Browser playback depends on your device and media format.

Do I need a powerful server?

Start with a compatible x86-64 Linux Docker host or NAS. Requirements vary with your media, concurrent viewers, and transcoding needs. CPU-only setup is supported; demanding formats may need more resources. Test with a small library first.

Is this a finished commercial service?

AlreadyOn is an open-source project inviting homelab testers. Expect rough edges, check release notes, and help us understand what works on your hardware. There is no guaranteed support response time.

What devices are supported?

The current focus is the WebUI, Android phones and tablets, and Google TV. Apple clients, Google Cast, and VPN-based access are outside the project’s current scope.
